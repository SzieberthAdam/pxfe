# 140. Version 23: the keys of the palette bar and the colour picker, on a duplicate of font 3b52125b (one colour, black), glyph U+0048.
# Plain '.', 0-9 and A-Z pick a slot (typed: `type 3`); the tools are Shift+letter; typing into a text field leaves the ink alone.
# '+' opens the picker; the colour chosen is added as an unused slot (dashed); drawing with it makes the glyph canonical again (it becomes
# slot 0 when its pixel comes first in reading order); a double-click on a slot changes its colour; each change is one undo step.
wait 5
click @Unlock
wait 4
dblclick @cell:U+0048
wait 5
assert editor 1
assert editor_glyph U+0048
assert epal 1
assert ebarrows 1
assert ebarextras 0
assert einkrgb 0x000000
assert eink '0
assert w.pal:. 1
assert w.pal:0 1
assert w.pal:+ 1
assert w.pal:1 0
