# 134. The Contour tool (version 20), font 0820dc69 unlocked, glyph U+0020 (empty, advance 4; font box rows 0..7).
# Key C and the strip's Contour: a freehand drag; on the release the path is closed and drawn with the pixel-perfect rule (the corner cells of its Ls go);
# with Filled the inside too (even-odd).  The drag (0,1) (3,1) (3,6) (0,6) (0,2) has 13 outline cells and 21 filled ones.
wait 5
click @Unlock
wait 4
sleep 600
dblclick @cell:U+0020
wait 5
assert editor_glyph U+0020
assert glyph U+0020 empty
assert undo 0

key c shift
wait 2
assert etool 13
assert w.Filled 1
assert efilled 0
key b shift
wait 2
click @Contour
wait 2
assert etool 13
