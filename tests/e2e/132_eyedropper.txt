# 132. The eyedropper (version 20), font 408c4d7d (colours 0 and 1; glyph U+0020), unlocked, as in 06b.
# Shift+I (version 23; I before) and the strip's Eyedropper pick the colour of the pixel under the cursor as the ink (version 22: inks are colours, so the asserts
# name the colour with einkrgb and the swatches by #RRGGBB, since the font's colour list reorders as the space gains colours); with the Eyedropper tool the right button picks too;
# '.' (empty space) is an ink of every font; Alt held at the click does the same for the Pencil, Line, Rectangle, Ellipse, Curve, Polygon, Contour and Fill.
wait 5
click @Unlock
wait 4
click @cell:U+0020
key enter
wait 5
assert editor 1
assert editor_glyph U+0020
assert eink '?
assert einkrgb 0x808080

# two pixels of different colours
click @pal:#FFFFFF
click @ecell:1,2
wait 3
click @pal:#808080
click @ecell:2,2
wait 3
assert glyph U+0020 color 1 2 FFFFFF
assert glyph U+0020 color 2 2 808080
assert undo 2
