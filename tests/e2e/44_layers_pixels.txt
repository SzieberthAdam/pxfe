# 44. What the layers draw on the canvas, pixel exact: colour, opacity, offset, order against the current glyph, and the hidden
# current glyph (editing blocked).  Duplicate of 0820dc69, glyph U+0020 (empty, advance 4); the layer is x (its row above the
# baseline is "0...0": cells (0,5) and (4,5)); the box rows 0..7, the pen at column 0.

click @Unlock
wait 4
click @cell:U+0020

wait 6

wait 3
click @+_Add_Layer
wait 3
click @lay_same
click @lay_glyph
type x
wait 2
click @Add
wait 3
assert l.count 2
assert l.color.0 0xE5484D

key enter
