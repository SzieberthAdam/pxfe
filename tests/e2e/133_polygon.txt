# 133. The Polygon tool (version 20), font 0820dc69 unlocked, glyph U+0020 (empty, advance 4; font box rows 0..7).
# Key D and the strip's Polygon; a click adds a vertex; Enter, a click on the last vertex again (a double click) or a click on the first vertex closes it;
# Esc cancels, Backspace takes the last vertex back; Shift makes each edge a clean angle; Filled adds the inside (even-odd); the right button draws '.'.
# The triangle (0,0) (3,0) (3,3) has 9 outline cells (the top row, the right column, the diagonal) and 10 cells when filled.
wait 5
click @Unlock
wait 4
sleep 600

wait 5

assert glyph U+0020 empty
assert undo 0

assert efilled 0
